# Bank 04: Supervisory memo

**Overall:** Sharp deterioration. The bank moved from the riskiest 34% to 12.7%, and now to 0.06%. It is mid-sized (60th size percentile).

**Main drivers:**
- **Losses:** ROA swung from +1.0% to -2.2% (1st percentile) in one quarter.
- **Capital erosion:** the leverage ratio fell from 10.9% to 9.0% to 5.9% (0.5th percentile). Equity/assets fell from 10.5% to 7.8%.
- **Fast growth on brokered funding:** deposits grew 64% to 96% a year (97th percentile or higher). Loans grew 21%. Brokered deposits are 12% to 24% of deposits.
- **Charge-offs:** charge-offs are persistently high at 0.36% of loans (93rd percentile). Noncurrent loans are only 0.31%, which is hard to square with the charge-offs.
- **Liquid assets:** securities are just 5% of assets and cash 2.4%.

**Lower concerns:** Uninsured deposits are 8%, commercial real estate is 83% of capital, and unrealized securities losses are small but rising (now 7% of Tier 1).

**Assessment:** A fast-growing bank funded by brokered deposits has just taken a large loss that consumed much of its capital.

**Questions for the supervisor:**
1. What caused this quarter's loss, and is it a one-off?
2. With leverage at 5.9%, what is the capital plan, and should growth and brokered funding be restricted?
3. Which loan segments are behind the high charge-offs, and are they the ones growing fastest?
