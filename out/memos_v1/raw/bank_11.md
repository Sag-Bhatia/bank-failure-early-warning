# Bank 11: Supervisory memo

**Overall:** Unremarkable by the model: the bank sits in the riskiest 56%, stable across the quarters. It is very large (99.6th size percentile). Credit is clean (noncurrent loans 0.19%, charge-offs 0.10%, commercial real estate 16% of capital), and ROA is about 1%.

**Risks the rank hides:**
- **Securities losses:** unrealized losses are 104% of Tier 1 (98.8th percentile), up from 80%. Securities are 56% of assets. Equity/assets is still 7.4%, which suggests much of the loss sits in held-to-maturity securities.
- **Uninsured deposits:** 86% of deposits (98.5th percentile).
- **Deposit outflow:** deposits are down 8.4% a year (6.6th percentile), after growing 29%.
- **Capital:** the leverage ratio is 8.0% (6th percentile).

**Why the score is low:** Large size, heavy securities holdings and a large uninsured base all carry risk-reducing weights in the model. Together, they describe a classic run vulnerability.

**Assessment:** The model's rank understates the risk. Liquidity and interest-rate risk need direct review.

**Questions for the supervisor:**
1. How are the securities losses split between available-for-sale and held-to-maturity, and what would forced sales do to capital?
2. How concentrated are the uninsured deposits, and what drove the outflow?
3. What borrowing capacity is secured against the securities book, and has it been tested?
