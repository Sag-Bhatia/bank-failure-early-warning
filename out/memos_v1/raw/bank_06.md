# Bank 06: Supervisory memo

**Overall:** Largely unremarkable. The bank is in the riskiest 15%, drifting slowly from 23%. It is very large (99.7th size percentile).

**Credit and earnings are sound:** noncurrent loans are 0.07% of loans, there are no net charge-offs, and ROA is a stable 0.85% to 0.88%. Commercial real estate is 211% of capital, close to the median.

**Score drivers:**
- Unrealized securities losses are 30% of Tier 1, stable at 24% to 35%. This is the largest push (+1.05).
- Capital is modest: the leverage ratio is 8.5% (13th percentile), flat.
- Cash fell from 3.2% to 2.0% of assets (14th percentile).

**Funding to watch:**
- Uninsured deposits are 68% of deposits (97th percentile).
- Brokered deposits rose from 0.4% to 3.0%.
- Loans grow about 23% a year, while deposit growth slowed from 23% to 13%.

**Assessment:** There are no acute problems. The drift in the ranking reflects lower cash and more wholesale funding for loan growth. Routine monitoring is enough.

**Questions for the supervisor:**
1. How will the bank fund 23% loan growth if deposit growth keeps slowing?
2. How concentrated are the uninsured deposits, and what liquid assets cover a stressed outflow?
