# Bank 02: Supervisory memo

**Overall:** High risk. The bank is consistently in the riskiest 0.4% (0.17% this quarter). It is small (15th size percentile).

**Main drivers:**
- **Negative equity:** equity/assets is -0.8% to -1.7%, the largest push (+2.6). The leverage ratio is still 9.3%, so the negative equity most likely comes from unrealized securities losses.
- **Securities losses:** unrealized losses are 119% of Tier 1 (99th percentile), up from 110%. Securities are 52% of assets.
- **Problem loans:** noncurrent loans plus foreclosed real estate are 4.9% of loans (96th percentile). Charge-offs rose from 0.01% to 0.18%.
- **Weak earnings:** ROA fell from 0.37% to 0.31% (9th percentile).

**Data caveats:** The Texas ratio of about 1,380 is meaningless because equity is negative; the model gives it almost no weight. Zero construction lending adds +0.59 to the score, which looks like a model artifact.

**Mitigants:** No brokered deposits, uninsured deposits at 23%, and cash at 9% of assets.

**Assessment:** Large securities losses, a material problem-loan book, and earnings too low to rebuild capital.

**Questions for the supervisor:**
1. How are the securities split between available-for-sale and held-to-maturity, and what is the plan to restore positive equity?
2. What makes up the 4.9% noncurrent book, and are reserves adequate?
3. Could the bank meet a moderate deposit outflow without selling securities at a loss?
