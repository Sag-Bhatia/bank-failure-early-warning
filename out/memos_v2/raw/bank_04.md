# Bank 04 – Supervisory memo

**Dominant risk: both, with run risk sharpening.** run_lens is 0.972, 0.964 and 0.979 from T-2 to T, and run_gap rose steeply from 3.92 to 5.008 to 8.099. Uninsured deposits are 47.4% (89.9th percentile) and cash only 3.8% of assets, although securities are 54.1%. mtm_cap is thin at 3.1%. credit_lens is also high (0.978, 0.923, 0.925): the Texas ratio is 12.9% and problem assets 1.58% of loans, both better than at T-2 (25.2% and 2.17%). Construction loans are 0.74 times capital (79.7th percentile). Equity is 4.57% of assets (5.5th percentile).

**Trend.** Credit improved slightly; liquidity worsened. Unrealized losses fell from 68.9% to 24.3% of Tier 1, yet run_gap kept rising, which points to shrinking unencumbered liquidity rather than market losses.

**Screens vs model.** The model is far calmer: rank 33.84, 44.82, 45.25, i.e. getting less risky. Strong ROA of 1.89% (-0.981), the large securities book (-0.832) and zero brokered deposits (-0.831) offset thin equity (1.246) and unrealized losses (0.988). It also scores the high uninsured share as lowering risk (-0.112). The model does not see the run_gap rise.
