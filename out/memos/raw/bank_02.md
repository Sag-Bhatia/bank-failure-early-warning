# Bank 02: supervisory memo

**Dominant risk: concentrated uninsured deposits funding long-dated securities.** The run lens is 0.992, 0.988 and 0.975 (T-2 to T) and the Depositors dimension about 0.99 throughout. Uninsured deposits are 86% (98.5th percentile), average large accounts $2.77M, and the run gap 3.74 (95.1st). Unrealized securities losses equal 85% of Tier 1 (97.9th) and long-dated assets 5.2x Tier 1. Borrowings are 11.7% of assets (92.8th).

**Trend.** Liquidity improved: the liquid-asset ratio went 0.4%, 10.8%, 18.6% and the run gap 163.76, 6.57, 3.74. The failure model eased from 0.813 to 0.668 to 0.515. Mark-to-market capital stays thin: 2.4%, 0.04%, 2.1%. Leverage is 8.73% (16.9th).

**Data gaps.** Loans/deposits is 0 and CRE and construction are 0; NPLs, charge-offs, reserve coverage, loan growth and swaps are not reported. The bank appears to hold essentially no loans, so the low asset-quality score (0.071) and credit lens (0.078) reflect the absence of a loan book, not strength. The T-2 run gap of 163.76 rests on a 0.4% liquid ratio and should be verified.

**Model disagreement.** The failure model, trained on credit failures, has little to read here and sits mid-range; the run lens, near the top, is the more relevant signal.

**Questions**
1. How concentrated are the large depositors, and how have their balances behaved under stress?
2. How much of the securities portfolio is unencumbered and could be borrowed against without realizing losses?
3. Can the bank confirm the T-2 liquidity figures and explain its business model with no loan book?
