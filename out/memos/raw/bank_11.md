# Bank 11: supervisory memo

**Dominant risk: uninsured deposit concentration, now with outflows.** Very large bank (99.3rd size percentile). Uninsured deposits are 90% (98.6th percentile), average large accounts $3.07M, and the run gap 2.56, 2.75, 3.08 (T-2 to T; 92.1st). Run lens steady at 0.857, 0.843, 0.847; Depositors dimension 0.994.

**Trend.** Deposit growth swung from 21.7% to 7.6% to -16.5% (1.8th percentile). Borrowings jumped from 1.5% to 1.4% to 10.4% of assets, brokered deposits rose to 4.3%, and loans/deposits rose from 70% to 72% to 85%. The failure model moved from 0.135 to 0.109 to 0.326.

**Other risks.** CRE is 350% of capital (76.8th). Tier 1 risk-based capital of 11.20% (8.7th) and leverage of 8.79% (17.9th) are below most peers. Credit quality is sound (NPLs+OREO 0.32%, Texas ratio 2.3%), mark-to-market capital is positive (2.3%), efficiency strong (32%), and long-dated assets modest (1.2x Tier 1).

**Model disagreement.** Large. The failure model, trained mostly on credit failures, sees clean loans and good earnings and stays low; the run lens flags the uninsured base. The deposit outflow and its replacement with borrowings are exactly what the failure model is not built to see.

**Questions**
1. Which depositors left, and how concentrated is the remaining uninsured base by client or sector?
2. What collateral backs the new borrowings, and how much capacity remains?
3. How would CRE losses at 350% of capital interact with a further deposit outflow?
