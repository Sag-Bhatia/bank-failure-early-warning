# Bank 06: supervisory memo

**Dominant risks: thin capital, large market-value losses, CRE concentration and uninsured funding, all worsening.** Failure model 0.974, 0.981, 0.997 (T-2 to T); run lens 0.961, 0.974, 0.984; credit lens 0.781, 0.832, 0.915.

**Capital and rate risk.** Leverage is 5.27% and Tier 1 risk-based 9.45% (both 0.5th percentile). Mark-to-market capital fell from -3.9% to -7.1% to -9.3% (0.1st). Unrealized securities losses equal 133% of Tier 1 and estimated loan losses at market prices 129%; long-dated assets are 10.2x Tier 1 (99.5th).

**Credit and growth.** CRE is 487% of capital (93.8th) and construction 80% (82.2nd), yet loans grew 25%. The Texas ratio rose from 4.1% to 4.5% to 5.1% (81.7th).

**Funding.** Uninsured deposits are 64% (96.7th). The run gap rose from 2.52 to 3.09 to 3.65; the liquid-asset ratio fell from 22% to 18% to 15%; borrowings rose from 5.0% to 7.4% to 12.2% of assets. ROA slid from 0.57% to 0.49% to 0.20%.

**Model agreement.** Both models agree and both worsened every quarter. This is a combined credit, rate and run vulnerability with little capital buffer.

**Questions**
1. Capital is deeply negative at market prices; what is the plan to raise capital or shrink the balance sheet?
2. Why is the bank growing CRE and construction lending 25% while its capital ratios are at the bottom of peers?
3. What borrowing capacity remains after the recent increase, and would it cover a significant uninsured outflow?
