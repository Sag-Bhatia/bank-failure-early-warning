# Bank 03: supervisory memo

**Dominant risk: uninsured deposits plus securities losses at a very large bank** (99.6th size percentile). Uninsured deposits are 86% (98.5th percentile) with average large accounts of $4.18M. Unrealized securities losses equal 104% of Tier 1 (98.8th) and long-dated assets 4.8x Tier 1. Leverage is 7.96% (6.2nd percentile) and mark-to-market capital is -0.95%.

**Trend: deteriorating liquidity.** Run lens 0.861, 0.865, 0.922 (T-2 to T); failure model 0.439, 0.448, 0.635. Deposit growth swung from 28.9% to 3.8% to -8.4%. The liquid-asset ratio fell from 59% to 52% to 36% and the run gap rose from 1.32 to 1.42 to 2.02. Borrowings rose from 1.75% to 6.45% to 7.45% of assets. Mark-to-market capital went from +1.25% to -1.24% to -0.95%.

**Credit is not the issue.** NPLs+OREO are 0.19%, the Texas ratio 0.79%, reserves cover problem loans 4.6x, and ROA is 0.96%.

**Model disagreement.** The run lens has been high for all three quarters; the failure model, trained on credit-driven failures, stayed mid-range and only rose this quarter, coinciding with the deposit outflow and liquidity drain. It still sits well below the run lens.

**Questions**
1. Which depositors are leaving, and what further outflow does the bank expect?
2. How much remaining liquidity is unencumbered and usable same-day, and what borrowing capacity is pre-positioned?
3. Would selling securities to meet outflows crystallize losses large enough to impair capital?
