# Bank 07: Supervisory memo

**Overall:** The data cannot be assessed. The bank ranks in the riskiest 0.02% in every quarter, but this comes from missing or zero values, not from evidence of risk.

**Data problems:**
- Equity/assets, noncurrent loans, the Texas ratio, real estate ratios, charge-offs, brokered deposits and unrealized losses are blank in all quarters.
- The leverage ratio, ROA, uninsured share, securities and cash are all exactly 0.

**Effect on the score:** These zeros drive it. Securities add +2.5, leverage +2.2, cash +2.0 and ROA +1.0. A bank with deposits cannot really hold no cash and no securities. The pattern suggests a special-purpose filer, an incomplete filing or a data mapping error.

**Usable figures:** Loans are shrinking 5% to 8% a year, deposits are roughly flat, and the bank is mid-sized (about 60th size percentile). None of these is alarming.

**Assessment:** Do not act on this score. First confirm the data, then rescore the bank.

**Questions for the supervisor:**
1. Is this a standard filer, or does it file reduced schedules (for example as a trust-only or special-purpose charter)?
2. Can the missing figures be obtained from the full filing so the bank can be rescored?
3. Should the model flag banks with this many missing inputs instead of ranking them as the riskiest?
