# Bank 07 – Supervisory memo

**Dominant risk: both, and everything is worsening.** This is the clearest case in the set. run_lens rose from 0.961 to 0.974 to 0.984 and run_gap from 3.002 to 4.185 to 5.262. Uninsured deposits are 63.9% (96.7th percentile); cash is 0.82% of assets (1.1st percentile); deposits fell 3.5% at T. mtm_cap went from 0.64% to -1.44% to -2.22%, and unrealized losses are 133.5% of Tier 1.

credit_lens rose from 0.781 to 0.832 to 0.915; the Texas ratio climbed from 5.8% to 11.8%. CRE is 4.87 times capital (93.8th percentile) and construction 0.80 times. Equity fell from 4.06% to 2.2% of assets, the leverage ratio is 5.27% (0.5th percentile) and ROA dropped from 0.57% to 0.20%.

**Screens vs model.** All three agree. The model ranks the bank in the riskiest 0.17%, 0.15% and 0.08%, driven by unrealized losses (1.836), low leverage (1.592), thin equity (1.538), weak ROA (0.805), low cash (0.795) and CRE (0.364). Its only offsets are size (-0.501), securities share (-0.589) and, oddly, the uninsured share (-0.143).
