# Bank 09: supervisory memo

**Dominant risks: a shrinking liquidity buffer against uninsured deposits, plus construction concentration.** Run lens 0.972, 0.964, 0.979 (T-2 to T). The liquid-asset ratio fell from 13% to 11% to 7.0% (4.5th percentile) and the run gap rose from 3.92 to 4.18 to 6.17 (98.7th). Uninsured deposits are 47% (89.9th) with average large accounts of $1.14M. Borrowings appeared this quarter at 3.6% of assets.

**Credit.** Construction loans are 74% of capital (79.7th) and CRE 164%. NPLs+OREO are 1.58% (88th), the Texas ratio 6.8% (87.1st), and reserve coverage 75% (8.2nd). Credit lens 0.978, 0.923, 0.925.

**Earnings and capital.** Earnings are a strength: ROA 1.89% (92.6th), efficiency 48%. Unrealized securities losses fell from 69% to 29% to 24% of Tier 1, and mark-to-market capital is near zero (-0.13%). Book equity/assets is low at 4.57% (5.5th) against 9.50% leverage.

**Model view.** Failure model 0.987, 0.898, 0.914. The models broadly agree; strong earnings may hold the failure model slightly below the run lens, which keys on the falling liquid buffer.

**Questions**
1. Why has the liquid-asset ratio fallen to 7%, and what contingent funding is in place?
2. How are the construction projects performing, and why do reserves cover only 75% of problem assets?
3. Who are the larger uninsured depositors, and are any of them linked to construction or CRE borrowers?
