# Bank 08: supervisory memo

**Dominant risks: rate exposure and wholesale funding, with a sudden credit jump this quarter.** The failure model is 0.998-0.999 in all three quarters. Long-dated assets are 7.8x Tier 1 (98.3rd percentile), unrealized securities losses 94% of Tier 1 (98.5th), and mark-to-market capital -3.3%. Brokered deposits are 28.6% (97.3rd), borrowings 8.5% of assets (85.9th), and swaps 18.7% of assets. Deposits grew 34% (95.3rd).

**Credit change.** The credit lens jumped from 0.076 to 0.299 to 0.972. NPLs+OREO went from 0.0% to 0.05% to 2.12% and the Texas ratio from 0.0% to 0.26% to 11.9% (94.6th). Reserve coverage fell to 56% (from 22.46x at T-1). The T-2 NPL figure of exactly zero, with reserve coverage not reported, should be checked.

**Earnings** are strong: ROA 1.24%, efficiency 49%.

**Model disagreement.** The failure model was already near the top before credit deteriorated, so it was reading capital, rate and funding structure rather than credit. The run lens is moderate (0.635, 0.666, 0.593) because uninsured deposits are only 18% and the run gap 0.47; it does not capture the risk that brokered funding fails to roll.

**Questions**
1. What caused problem assets to rise from 0.05% to 2.12% in one quarter, and is the reserve adequate?
2. How much brokered funding matures within a year, and could the bank replace it if credit losses reduce capital?
3. What do the swaps hedge, and how much of the long-dated exposure do they offset?
